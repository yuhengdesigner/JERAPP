Johor Emergency Response

JER is an emergency response platform developed for Johor citizens and tourists to quickly request assistance from Police, Medical and Fire departments without relying solely on traditional emergency calls.

About JER

JER (Johor Emergency Response) is a mobile application designed to improve emergency communication between the public and emergency service departments. Users can submit emergency requests, share their location, upload evidence videos, track responder status, and access emergency education resources such as CPR tutorials and safety procedures.

Key Features

Emergency Request

Quickly send emergency alerts to Police, Medical, or Fire departments.

Live Location Tracking

Share your current coordinates and monitor responder progress through map integration.

Video Evidence Upload

Record and submit incident videos directly to department administrators.

Emergency History

Review previous emergency requests and their status.

Offline Tutorials

Access CPR guidance, emergency procedures and safety information even without internet access.

SOS Flashlight

Use built-in SOS signaling tools during critical situations.

How JER Works

1. Access the App

Register, log in, or continue as a guest user.

2. Select Emergency Department

Choose Police, Medical or Fire services and filter nearby departments.

3. Confirm Emergency

Submit your emergency request and send your location to the department.

4. Track Assistance

Monitor emergency response progress and responder location.

5. Upload Additional Evidence

Record videos and share information with responders.

6. Emergency Completed

Close the case after responders arrive and review it later in your emergency history.
